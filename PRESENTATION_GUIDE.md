## 🎥 Demo Flow

### 1. Introduction & Overview (1 minute)
**"This is our AI Injury Risk Predictor - a comprehensive system that analyzes movement patterns in real-time."**

- Show the main interface
- Point out the two analysis modes: Live Camera and Video Analysis
- Highlight the professional medical-grade design

### 2. Live Camera Demo (3-4 minutes)

**"Let's start with live analysis using my camera."**

#### Setup:
1. Click **"Live Camera"** tab
2. Select **"Squat"** exercise
3. Click **"Start Analysis"**
4. Allow camera permissions

#### Demo Script:
**"Watch as I perform a squat. The system analyzes my movement in real-time."**

- Perform 3-5 squats in front of camera
- **Point out real-time metrics** updating every 100ms
- **Highlight the risk percentage** changing
- **Show the color-coded feedback** (green/yellow/red)
- **Read specific recommendations** from the feedback panel

**Key Talking Points:**
- "Notice how the knee valgus angle changes as I adjust my form"
- "The system provides specific recommendations like 'strengthen glutes' or 'improve ankle mobility'"
- "This immediate feedback helps prevent injuries before they happen"

### 3. Video Analysis Demo - Perfect Form (2 minutes)

**"Now let's analyze some pre-recorded examples to see how the system handles different movement qualities."**

#### Setup:
1. Click **"Video Analysis"** tab
2. Select **"Perfect Squat Form - Low Risk"**
3. Click **"Start Analysis"**

#### Demo Script:
**"This demonstrates excellent squat technique."**

- Watch the risk assessment stay low (green)
- Point out high-quality metrics (90%+ symmetry, minimal knee valgus)
- Show positive feedback messages
- **Key Point**: "Notice how the system recognizes good form with low injury risk"

### 4. Video Analysis Demo - Poor Form (2 minutes)

**"Now let's see what happens with poor technique."**

#### Setup:
1. Select **"Poor Squat Form - High Risk"**
2. Click **"Start Analysis"**

#### Demo Script:
**"Watch how the system immediately detects movement issues."**

- Point out high risk percentage (red indicators)
- Show concerning metrics (high knee valgus, forward lean)
- Read specific corrective recommendations
- **Key Point**: "The AI identifies specific problems and provides actionable solutions"

### 5. Exercise Variety (1 minute)

**"The system supports 6 different exercises, each with specialized analysis."**

- Quickly show the exercise dropdown
- Mention: Squat, Jump Landing, Lunge, Overhead Press, Deadlift, Running
- **Key Point**: "Each exercise has unique biomechanical risk factors"
